Distance courses

Master of Divinity (M.Div)

A four-year, post-graduate level programme accredited by the Asia Theological Association (ATA), comprising 30 subjects across Bible, Theology, Ethics, Church History, Religion, and Christian Ministry.

Strong foundation

Emphasis on solid biblical grounding paired with theological reflection and practical application.

Self-study materials

Comprehensive, purpose-built study materials so you can learn at your own pace.

Local tutors

Tutors guide your study and conduct exams close to your own city or place of ministry.

Flexible pace

Up to six years to complete the programme, fitted around your existing commitments.

Medium of study

Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

Further study

A pathway into M.Th and, beyond that, PhD studies.

Who can apply

Eligibility &
entrance requirements.

All applicants sit an entrance examination in three papers: English, General Knowledge, and Bible Knowledge. Additional requirements depend on your background.

  1. University graduates & B.Th holders

    A university degree or B.Th qualifies you to apply directly.

  2. Mature candidates (30+)

    H.S.C. or a 10+2 diploma is accepted from age 30 and above, provided you also pass two qualifying papers: History of India since 1857, and Indian Religions and Culture.

  3. B.Th upgraders

    A B grade (second class) or above from an ATA-accredited college or the Senate of Serampore is credited toward your B.Th coursework already completed.

  4. B.Th with B- or below

    Three additional qualifying papers are required: Christian Ministry, Christian Theology, and Biblical Studies.

On campus

A short
residential session.

Distance students attend contact classes on campus once a year, for five days, across four consecutive years. Upgraders take part in only two such sessions, and foreign candidates are excluded from the residential requirement entirely.
