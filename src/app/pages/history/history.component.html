MILESTONE 1993

The Celebration of the 40th Anniversary

The celebration of the 40th anniversary was a milestone in the history of UBS. Two full days, 8–9 March 1993, were set apart by the UBS Alumni Association (UBSAA) to recall and celebrate the goodness of the Lord in the existence of UBS.

One hundred and twenty graduates, some with their families, came to UBS with a deep sense of gratitude for God's abiding faithfulness.

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

Theology that reaches the community.

UBS has trained many girls and women from nearby slums, giving them skills in income-generating activities. The Seminary seeks to extend this project to meet the needs of young men as well.

A new building is being planned for this purpose, with support made possible through World Hope.

WORD & DEED
PRIMARY SCHOOL INITIATIVE

Preparing the whole church.

The ground breaking ceremony was held for starting a primary school for poor children.

In today's world, theological education must go beyond training pastors and evangelists. We must prepare the whole church to participate in the whole of society in "word and deed".

COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP

Tyrannus Hall International

Another way of reaching out to the community around UBS is the relationship forged with Tyrannus Hall International.

This ministry gives hostel facilities to students studying in colleges and universities while nurturing them in the love of Jesus Christ.

It is a discipling ministry with a hope to equip them to become fruitful witnesses in their places of work in future.
