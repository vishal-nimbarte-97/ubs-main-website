Which programmes does UBS offer?

UBS offers residential, short-term, and non-residential theological education pathways.

How do I apply?

Begin with the Apply page, choose your programme, and contact the admissions office for the current application requirements.

Where can I find tuition information?

Visit the Tuition page or contact the seminary for the current fee schedule and related costs.

Can I study while serving in ministry?

Yes. Non-residential programmes are designed for learners balancing theological study with ministry and work.
