— Campus Life at UBS

Where Study, Worship And Community Meet

Set in Bibwewadi, Pune, the UBS campus is shaped by nature and the rhythm of shared life — a place for focused study, unhurried worship, and everyday community, held together by green grounds and modern learning spaces.

— Community Life

A family,
before it is an institution.

Students, faculty and staff share more than classrooms here — meals, responsibilities, and friendships that carry on well past graduation. It's a close-knit rhythm of learning, fellowship and service.

Growth at UBS is holistic. Everyday campus duties and environmental care sit alongside the curriculum, building teamwork and stewardship as much as scholarship. Shared guidelines keep that life together safe, respectful and welcoming for everyone on campus.

A rhythm of study, worship, friendship and life — shared by every class that has called this campus home.

— Community Activities

Life outside the classroom

Evenings on campus mean the sports ground, most days from 5–7 p.m. On Independence Day and Republic Day, the flag-hoisting gives way to friendly matches that pull the whole community outdoors. The student council, guided by faculty mentors, coordinates everything social and cultural, keeping campus life vibrant and inclusive for every class.
