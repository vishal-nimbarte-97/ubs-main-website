The UBS Library is an essential academic resource supporting students, faculty and researchers with access to books, reference materials, archives and digital resources.

Designed as a quiet and focused environment, the library provides spaces where knowledge, research and independent learning can flourish.

HERITAGE & HISTORY

Preserving the story of UBS.

The Archives are located on the first floor and serve as the official repository for historical records, papers, documents and materials related to the Seminary.

The collection spans the history of the Seminary and includes photographic archives, audio-visual materials, manuscripts and documents of historical significance.
